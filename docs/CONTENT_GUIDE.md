# Content guide

## Primary proposition

**Make technology decidable.**

Moderneer gives enterprise technology leaders a continuously evidence-backed way to understand what is really happening, decide what matters, and act with confidence.

## Product hierarchy

- Moderneer: Outcome Engineering
- Telos Studio: Executive decision intelligence

## Terms to use

- Outcome Engineering
- enterprise evidence
- executive judgement
- Decision Agenda
- evidence basis
- confidence rationale
- validation gap
- recommended next move
- Fix Now / Invest / Assure

## Terms to avoid in top-level marketing

- Edge as a standalone public product
- internal pipeline names
- implementation-specific storage technology
- speculative ROI
- unverified customer claims
