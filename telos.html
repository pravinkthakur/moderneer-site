02Build the signal context

Connect what the decision needs, not everything you own.

Telos can assemble evidence from operational, commercial, technical, documentary and external sources. The point is not to create another data lake. It is to create enough trusted context for the decision.

Current packaged connectors are shown here. The evidence model is broader than any one connector set, but we do not claim every enterprise source is pre-integrated today.

04Know what to trust

Evidence quality changes the decision.

Mirror turns trust into something visible and discussable. Confidence, reliability and integrity are scored separately so a persuasive-looking answer cannot silently outrun the evidence behind it.

Confidence

How strongly does the evidence support the claim?

Reliability

Do independent sources agree?

Integrity

Is the evidence traceable, fresh and defensible?

Expected benefits, success criteria, target metrics, measurement methods and repeat runs let the organisation compare what it expected with what actually happened.
