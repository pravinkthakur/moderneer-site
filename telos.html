Three operating modes

Not every finding should be treated the same way.

Fix Now

Reduce material exposure.

Immediate actions for significant security, reliability, delivery, architecture or operating risks.

Invest

Build strategic capability.

Longer-horizon improvements where targeted investment can materially improve performance or resilience.

Assure

Protect valuable strengths.

Validate and preserve the controls, capabilities and practices already producing positive outcomes.

Executive trust

Telos separates evidence from inference.

Evidence strength

Know what the decision is standing on.

Evidence can be strong, moderate or weak, with the rationale available for review rather than hidden behind a score.

Decision readiness

Do not pretend a recommendation is ready when it is not.

Telos distinguishes ready-to-decide from needs-validation, needs-evidence and not-ready states, and adjusts the next move accordingly.

Alternatives & trade-offs

Show credible paths, not a single AI answer.

Decision options include material differences, likely upside, likely downside and the conditions under which an alternative may be more appropriate.

Measurement

Tie the decision to outcomes.

Expected benefits, claim metrics, measurement windows and success criteria keep the decision connected to what should change after action is taken.
