04Know what to trust

Evidence quality changes what can be concluded.

Mirror turns trust into something visible and discussable. Confidence, reliability and integrity are scored separately so a persuasive-looking answer cannot silently outrun the evidence behind it.

Confidence

How strongly does the evidence support the claim?

Reliability

Do independent sources agree?

Integrity

Is the evidence traceable, fresh and defensible?

05Derive the Decision Agenda

Surface the decisions the evidence says leadership needs to make.

Telos synthesises Fix Now, Invest and Assure recommendations, decision options, executive considerations and linked risks into executive decision themes. Run priorities focus this synthesis; the decision themes emerge from the assessed evidence. Why now, evidence strength, confidence, the main gap, readiness and the next move remain visible.

06Support executive judgement

Do not confuse a recommendation with a decision.

Telos does not make the executive decision. It makes the judgement more explainable by showing credible paths, trade-offs, consequences, evidence gaps and what happens if the organisation does nothing.

Build internallyBuyPartnerPilotScale existingDefer & monitorCommunicate / alignHybrid

08Ask the evidence

Natural-language exploration without losing the run context.

Telos chat is not a detached general assistant. It is attached to the evidence and metadata of the selected run, with collaboration features such as participants and mentions.

What evidence most weakens this recommendation?Which assumption has the highest downside if wrong?What changed since the previous run?Show me the evidence behind this decision theme.
09Learn over time

Make the decision testable.

Expected benefits, success criteria, target metrics, measurement methods and repeat runs let the organisation compare what it expected with what actually happened.

That closes the loop from judgement into Outcome Engineering: what was chosen, what was committed, what changed and what the new evidence now says.
