Enterprise trust

The AI is not the source of truth. The enterprise evidence is.

AI helps interpret and synthesise evidence. It should not erase provenance, hide uncertainty or take accountability away from leaders.

01
Evidence provenance

Recommendations and decisions remain linked back to the findings, risks, considerations and options that support them.

02
Visible uncertainty

Telos distinguishes direct, inferred, mixed, missing and contradictory evidence, alongside explicit decision-readiness states.

03
Controlled model use

Moderneer is designed for structured evidence handling and explicit control over model/provider usage, including privacy-sensitive deployment patterns.

04
Human accountability

Leaders see assumptions, blind spots and trade-offs and retain ownership of the material decision.
