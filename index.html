The Moderneer model

One continuous line from question to outcome.

Decision Engineering makes the reasoning explicit. Outcome Engineering closes the loop by checking whether the decision actually produced the intended result.

01Frame

Define the decision, audience, scope, horizon and considerations.

02Connect

Identify and bring together the signals relevant to the question.

03Trust

Assess coverage, freshness, provenance, agreement and gaps.

04Understand

Find patterns, risks, strengths, relationships and constraints.

05Decide

Compare paths, trade-offs, consequences and readiness.

06Commit

Turn the chosen next move into owned action.

07Learn

Measure outcomes and let new evidence update the position.

Evidence before intelligence

A signal is not automatically evidence.

Moderneer separates the existence of data from the strength of the evidence it provides. Trust is part of the decision model, not a disclaimer added at the end.
