The Moderneer model

One continuous line from priority to action and reassessment.

Moderneer makes the path from evidence to judgement explicit. Outcome Engineering carries judgement into accountable commitments and repeat runs, so new evidence can test whether the intended outcome is being realised.

Evidence before intelligence

A signal is not automatically evidence.

Moderneer separates the existence of data from the strength of the evidence it provides. Confidence, reliability and integrity affect what can be concluded and whether leadership should decide, validate or gather more evidence.
