# Moderneer Site

Official marketing site for **Moderneer** and **Telos Studio**.

## Canonical positioning

- **Moderneer** — a reusable operating judgement layer that connects fragmented enterprise evidence, interprets it and surfaces what matters.
- **Telos Studio** — the **System of Judgement** where assessments, recommendations, decision options and executive considerations are synthesised into decision themes leaders can challenge and judge.
- **Outcome Engineering** — the follow-through loop from executive judgement to commitment, measurable outcome and reassessment.
- **Technology & Transformation Intelligence** — the deepest packaged application today, with 12 pillars and 365+ evidence checks.

## Canonical product story

**Leadership context → relevant evidence → assessment → trust → recommendations / options / considerations → derived Decision Agenda → executive judgement → commitment → learning**

New Run starts with evidence-source configuration and selected executive considerations. These define run priorities; the Decision Agenda emerges from the assessment.

Canonical rule:

**Priorities focus the assessment. Evidence surfaces the decisions. Leadership makes the judgement.**

## Homepage narrative — the 10-minute pitch

**Evidence-rich / judgement-poor → System of Judgement → set run priorities → connect / assess / trust → synthesise decision themes → support executive judgement → track commitments → Technology & Transformation proof point → 30-day Transformation Baseline.**

## Current GTM entry point

The March 2026 GTM deck describes a **30-day Transformation Baseline** on one critical transformation, modernisation or AI initiative. It is the current land motion; it is not presented as the limit of the platform.

## Product truth principles

- Do not say Moderneer or Telos "starts with the decision". It starts with run priorities, selected considerations and relevant evidence sources.
- Decision Agenda themes are derived from analysed evidence, recommendations, decision options and executive considerations.
- Telos supports executive judgement; it does not make the executive decision.
- Systems of record remain the source of truth.
- Confidence, reliability, integrity, evidence type and decision readiness remain visible.
- Keep Technology & Transformation as the deepest packaged application, not the boundary of the platform.
- Treat cross-functional examples outside the packaged application as illustrative unless the implementation explicitly supports them.
- Competitive intelligence and meeting/communication intelligence should not be marketed as mature current capabilities merely because they appear in product direction.
- Avoid invented customers, ROI, certifications or unsupported integrations.

## Deployment

The static site deploys from `main` through `.github/workflows/pages.yml` to `https://moderneer.co.uk/`.

## UX QA standards

- Core content remains visible without JavaScript.
- Important product labels have a 12px minimum readability floor.
- Native system fonts avoid third-party font-loading failures.
- Hero and product visuals use stable responsive layouts.
- The homepage is the 10-minute executive story; Telos is the 30-minute product deep dive.
